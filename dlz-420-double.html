Overview

Product Overview

The DLZ-420/520 Double-Sided Forming model represents ALLTOVAC's high-capacity solution, featuring dual-cavity simultaneous forming technology that effectively doubles production output per cycle. Both sides operate independently with synchronized sealing and cutting — ideal for high-volume manufacturers.

Built on ALLTOVAC's proven platform with interchangeable multi-mold configurations, support for soft film, hard film, and aluminum foil, and the capability to handle multi-step variable pitch. The 304 stainless steel main beam and Schneider PLC ensure unparalleled reliability.

Features

Key Features

🔀

Dual-Sided Simultaneous Forming

Two cavities formed per cycle — doubles throughput without increasing cycle speed. Independent sealing and cutting stations for each side ensure consistent quality.

📐

Multi-Film Compatibility

Seamlessly handles soft film, hard film, and aluminum foil. Multi-step variable pitch adjustment for diverse packaging formats and product sizes.

🔄

Quick Interchangeable Molds

Multiple mold sets can be swapped rapidly. Configure different product formats on each side for maximum production flexibility.

💨

Advanced Gas Flushing

Vacuum, Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP), and nitrogen flush modes available. Preserve product freshness and extend shelf life significantly.

🛡️

304 SS Hydroformed Construction

Main beam and critical structural components in 304 stainless steel — superior hygiene, corrosion resistance, and structural integrity for demanding environments.

📊

Production Data Management

Integrated production statistics, fault logging, and recipe management system. Multi-language HMI for international operator teams.
