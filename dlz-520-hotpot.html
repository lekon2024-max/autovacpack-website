Overview

Product Overview

The DLZ-520/560/620 Hot Pot series is specifically designed for the high-volume, standardized packaging requirements of hot pot ingredient producers. Equipped with integrated automatic weighing and automatic loading systems, this machine achieves true unmanned quantitative packaging — dramatically reducing labor costs while ensuring consistent portion control.

Capable of handling various hot pot ingredients including sliced meats, meatballs, seafood, vegetables, and tofu products. Supports soft film, hard film, and aluminum foil with multi-step variable pitch for different tray depths and formats. Custom molds available for any ingredient shape and size.

Features

Key Features

⚖️

Auto Weighing System

Integrated multi-head automatic weighing system ensures precise portion control (±1–2g) for every package. Ideal for standardized retail packs and food service portions.

🤖

Unmanned Auto Loading

Automatic vibratory and robotic loading system eliminates manual filling. Continuous unmanned operation for maximum labor savings and hygiene.

📦

Multiple Tray Formats

Custom mold design for various hot pot ingredients — sliced meat trays, meatball packs, combination platters, and seafood assortments. Quick mold changeover.

🌡️

MAP & Vacuum Options

Both vacuum skin packaging and Modified Atmosphere Packaging modes available. Nitrogen flush preserves color and freshness for premium product presentation.

🔧

Ingredient Versatility

Handles frozen sliced meat, meatballs, fish balls, tofu, vegetables, seafood, and mixed combinations. Adjustable forming depth for different product thicknesses.

📊

Production Analytics

Real-time production counting, weight statistics, and efficiency tracking. Integrated with weighing system for total traceability and quality control.
