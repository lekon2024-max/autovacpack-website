Overview

Product Overview

The DLZ-520/560/620 Sausage-Specialty series is precision-engineered for the high-speed, multi-count packaging of sausages and processed meat products. Optimized cavity spacing and forming depth are specifically designed for Taiwan-style sausages, frankfurters, cocktail sausages, and similar cylindrical products — ensuring perfect alignment and presentation in every pack.

Features include automatic counting and weighing integration, high-capacity multi-cavity molds (up to 50 sausages per cycle), and compatibility with vacuum, MAP, and skin packaging. The 304 stainless steel construction ensures food-grade hygiene for direct meat contact applications.

Features

Key Features

🌭

Sausage-Optimized Molds

Precision-engineered cavity design for perfect sausage alignment and presentation. Support for various sausage diameters and lengths — Taiwan-style, frankfurters, mini sausages.

⚡

High-Speed Multi-Count

Up to 80 sausages per cycle with automatic counting verification. Multi-cavity molds with synchronized filling for consistent pack weights and counts.

Retail-Ready Packaging

Integrated easy-tear notch punching, hang-hole creation, and inkjet coding. Produce supermarket-ready packs directly from the machine — no secondary processing needed.

Vacuum and Modified Atmosphere Packaging modes. Nitrogen flushing preserves sausage color and prevents oxidation — significantly extending retail shelf life.

Quick-disassembly forming and sealing stations for thorough sanitation. 304 stainless steel throughout — fully washdown-capable for meat processing environments.

Configurable for 5-pack, 10-pack, 20-pack, 30-pack, and 50-pack formats. Quick mold change enables format switching in minutes for SKU flexibility.
