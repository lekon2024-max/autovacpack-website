Overview

Product Overview

The DLZ-320/420/520 series is ALLTOVAC's flagship fully automatic thermoformer vacuum packaging machine, integrating stretch forming, material filling, vacuum sealing, gas flushing, and cutting into one continuous process. Adopting advanced manufacturing technology with French Schneider servo PLC control, this machine delivers exceptional reliability, speed, and packaging quality.

The main beam is upgraded from traditional aluminum alloy to hydroformed 304 stainless steel — an industry-first innovation that significantly enhances safety, aesthetics, and corrosion resistance. Suitable for 24/7 continuous production environments.

Features

Key Features

🎯

Precision Servo Control

French Schneider servo motors with PLC for precise film tracking, accurate forming and cutting registration. Supports color film and clear film with optical sensors.

🛡️

304 SS Hydroformed Main Beam

Industry-first 304 stainless steel hydroformed main beam replacing aluminum alloy — superior strength, hygiene, and corrosion resistance for food-grade environments.

⚡

Quick Mold Change

Tool-free quick-change mold system supporting up to 3 different mold configurations. Switch between product formats in minutes, not hours.

🌡️

Vacuum + MAP Capable

Supports vacuum packaging, Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP), and nitrogen flushing. Extends product shelf life while maintaining appearance.

♻️

Waste Film Recycling

Torque-adjustable automatic waste film winding system keeps the production area clean and enables film material recycling.

🖥️

Intelligent Touch Screen

User-friendly HMI touch screen with multi-language support, fault diagnostics, production statistics, and recipe management for different products.
