About Us

About ALLTOVAC

Creating value for our customers is the goal we relentlessly pursue

AutoVAC

ALLTOVAC is a professional equipment operator integrating conceptual design, R&D innovation, manufacturing, marketing, and service. Located in Zhucheng Economic Development Zone, Shandong Province, China, we specialize in the development and manufacturing of fully automatic thermoforming packaging machines — covering Vacuum, MAP (Modified Atmosphere Packaging), and VSP (Vacuum Skin Packaging) technologies.

We operate intelligent production workshops with automated manufacturing lines, built to German engineering standards and precision craftsmanship. Every machine undergoes rigorous quality control to deliver premium, cost-effective packaging solutions to our customers worldwide.

Our Mission: To advance the intelligent transformation of food industry equipment, creating unparalleled value through professionalism and service.

304 Stainless Steel Main Beam

Industry-first upgrade from aluminum alloy to hydroformed 304 stainless steel — superior safety, aesthetics, and corrosion resistance.
